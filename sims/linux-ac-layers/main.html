Even one DENY is enough; the checks are AND-composed. DAC (file permissions), Linux capabilities, and MAC (AppArmor/SELinux) are each consulted in turn. If DAC permits but MAC denies, MAC wins. The shadow read fails at the very first gate; the index.html read clears all three.
